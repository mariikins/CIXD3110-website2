01 / INTRODUCTION

MORE THAN
A PARK.

In the heart of Washington, D.C., Meridian Hill Park is a landscape where everyday life and history overlap. Known officially as Meridian Hill Park and commonly as Malcolm X Park, the space has become a gathering place for music, movement, protest, community, and quiet reflection.

Its long central axis, cascading fountain, open lawns, trees, stairs, and gathering spaces create a landscape that constantly changes depending on who occupies it.

02 / TIMELINE

LAYERS
OF TIME

1791

THE MERIDIAN

The land's name connects to Pierre L'Enfant's plan for Washington and the city's geographic meridian.

1900s

THE LANDSCAPE

The area is transformed into a formal urban park with terraces, gardens, pathways, and the monumental cascading fountain.

1960s

A NEW NAME

During the Civil Rights era, the park becomes increasingly associated with Malcolm X and is popularly called Malcolm X Park.

TODAY

A LIVING SPACE

Residents, musicians, dancers, families, visitors, and community groups continue to give the park new meanings.
